    Insight 1 · theme 1 resolved

    It was a naming problem.

    Split "ending" from "win condition". The ending is IN v1: a Life-at-20 summary, the top-3 decisions and replay-from-fork. The win condition and the wellbeing meter stay OUT.

    UJ-5 stays, rewritten to that shape. An optional cheap goal is a letter from your 15-year-old self, checked at 20, with no meter.

    Endgame at 20
    Insight 2 · the hidden link

    One requirement, two hats.

    One engine mechanism, a causal-link id on every effect, powers echo tags, the "Why?" tap, the top-3 decisions, the counterfactual lines and the ending screen. Themes 1 and 2 are one requirement wearing two hats. It is a candidate FR, plus new tests F9 and F10.

    Insight 3 · theme 3

    One schema to hold every event.

    Each event has a mechanism, an age window, requires/sets flags, boomerang pairs, a teaching_point, an LK20 tag and a source.

    ~30minimum for F6
    60-80target before playtest
    ~10crisis sets
    55/45pre/post-18 by months, with post-18 events heavier

    Before 18, "debt" is only informal: a friend lends you money.

    Event library
    Insight 4 · theme 4, via Five Whys

    We need persistence, not identity.

    Directions: anonymous sign-in or a resume code (email optional), no free-text input, never ask for a real age, playtest with 18-20 only, and keep playtest data on a separate anonymous form.

    Still open: a legal check of the Norwegian digital consent age.

    Privacy & consent
    Insight 5 · cross-theme tension

    Privacy decides the measurement.

    Banning free text (privacy) decides how the 70% causality criterion is measured: a multiple-choice in-app question at 20 plus a spoken debrief, not typed answers.
