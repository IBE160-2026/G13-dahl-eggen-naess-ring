## 🎯 Design Challenge

> *How might we help young people aged 15–20 understand how today's money decisions shape their options months and years later, before those decisions cost them real money?*

- **Users:** Young people aged 15–20 in Norway, especially those close to their first real money decisions (first job, bank card, credit at 18). Secondary: parents and teachers.
- **Constraints:** Student team, working first version due December 2026, zero running cost (free-tier AI), minors as users, minimal personal data.
- **Success:** Users can explain how an earlier decision affected a later outcome, and the learning feels self-chosen rather than like schoolwork.
- **Existing research:** Finans Norge 2025, market research and competitor table in the brief. No primary user data yet.
- **Research rule:** The solution idea (AI-driven money game) is not revealed or tested in the problem phase. The survey and interviews investigate the problem only.
