# D2 national guidance & initiatives — round 1 — digest 1
accessed: 2026-10-27

- claim: First Norwegian participation in PISA financial literacy (2022): Norwegian students score below OECD participating-country average; 22.8% low level, 10.5% high level; among largest low/high gaps; girls slightly better; smaller SES gap than other countries. ~8,500 15-year-olds. 79% say they learned about money in maths, 38% samfunnsfag. Students feel safe with digital transactions but not with bank forms, account statements, purchase contracts; few compare prices. 71% talk to parents weekly/monthly about money decisions. Few know "renters rente".
