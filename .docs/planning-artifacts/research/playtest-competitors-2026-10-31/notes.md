# Playtest notes: competitors

Hands-on notes from the team, as asked for in issue #13. These are first impressions from playing, not a systematic review. The whole team has tried both products (reported 2026-11-03).

## Koll på cashen (Finansinspektionen + Riksbanken, 2025)

- **Not very rewarding, heavy and slow.** The team wants LifeMode to be more entertaining and smoother to play.
- **Visual style is too childish for LifeMode's audience.** LifeMode needs a different, more grown-up graphic style that 15-20-year-olds take seriously. This goes into the UX work (DESIGN.md) as a deliberate contrast.
- **Differentiation: wellbeing must be tied directly into the economy.** In LifeMode, good personal finance is not only about ending up with the most money, but also about how the money is used. The happiness/wellbeing side should be built directly into the loop and the outcome, so that hoarding money at the cost of wellbeing is not a "win".

### Answered by desk research
- Money does carry over between Koll på cashen's four life phases (savings and income), but the home deposit is fixed at 15% of the price. No wellbeing mechanic. See `research/remaining-pre-prd-research-2026-10-31/research.md`.

## PersonalFinanceLab

- **Professional, but not made for the Norwegian market.**
- **Geared towards a teacher-led experience.** LifeMode is designed for the player.
- **It costs money.**

## Consequences for planning

- **Endgame (#19, theme 1):** settled. Wellbeing is part of the game: stress, free time and social life, with no housing in version one (team decisions 2026-11-03, see the PRD `.memlog.md`).
- **Differentiation claim (#12):** settled. The PRD keeps the full claim from `market-research.md`: "Built for Norway: a personal-finance life game for 15-20-year-olds, based on Norwegian rules and life stages, that combines good money management with wellbeing without becoming complicated."
- **UX:** the graphic style should be explicitly less childish than Koll på cashen, and play should feel smoother and more entertaining.
