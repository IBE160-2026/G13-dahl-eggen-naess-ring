# Playtest notes: competitors

Hands-on notes from the team, as asked for in issue #13. These are first impressions from playing, not a systematic review.

## Koll på cashen (Finansinspektionen + Riksbanken, 2025)

Played by Myggsaa on 2026-10-31.

- **Visual style is too childish for LifeMode's audience.** LifeMode needs a different, more grown-up graphic style that 15-20-year-olds take seriously. This goes into the UX work (DESIGN.md) as a deliberate contrast.
- **Differentiation: wellbeing must be tied directly into the economy.** In LifeMode, good personal finance is not only about ending up with the most money, but also about how the money is used. The happiness/wellbeing side (free time, social life, stress, housing) should be built directly into the loop and the outcome, so that hoarding money at the cost of wellbeing is not a "win".

## Consequences for planning

- **Endgame (#19, theme 1):** the brainstorm on 2026-10-29 recommended keeping the wellbeing meter out of scope for version one. This finding points the other way: wellbeing is now a core differentiator. The team must settle this before the PRD.
- **Differentiation claim (#12):** "money and how you use it, not just how much you have" is a candidate for LifeMode's narrower differentiation statement. Status: kept open for the team on purpose, but Myggsaa expects the team to land on it, because it makes LifeMode more distinctive.
- **UX:** the graphic style should be explicitly less childish than Koll på cashen.
