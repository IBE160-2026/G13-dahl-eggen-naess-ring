## Bottom line (assistant)
No binding age-rating or child-protection rule found that limits these events or stops a 15-year-old from playing. Rests on the assistant's legal inferences, not a regulator's statement.
- PEGI: voluntary and contract-based. If rated, PEGI's text points to at least PEGI 16, even with educational purpose or a refuse option (no published exception). A recommendation, not a legal bar.
- Bildeprogramloven: does not reach games.
- Markedsføringsloven: depends on commercial activity. The test is "næringsdrivende", not "free".
- Weak: Norwegian DSA status unconfirmed; DSA and AVMSD operative articles and PEGI assessment form not read.

## Q1. PEGI

- C1.2 Drugs descriptor: "The game refers to or depicts the use of illegal drugs, alcohol or tobacco." "Games with this content descriptor are always PEGI 16 or PEGI 18." Same page | high
- C1.3 Non-glamorised tobacco or alcohol appears to land at 16, not 18. PEGI 18 wording: "The glamorisation of the use of illegal drugs". Secondary source extends to "controlled substances", so glamorising may carry 18 risk. Same page; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PEGI (search extract) | medium | inference
- C1.4 No published text treats an in-game choice differently from showing use. "Refers to" could catch a refused offer (assistant's reading). Same page | low to medium | absence plus inference; assessment form not read
- C1.5 No educational exemption found. Code Art. 4.5 concerns educators' awareness only. Same page; https://pegi.info/pegi-code-of-conduct | PEGI / Video Games Europe | n/s | medium | absence
- C1.6 PEGI voluntary in Norway, contractual for those who join. Code: "a set of rules to which every publisher using the PEGI system is contractually committed"; Art. 1.1 binds publishers "that enter into an agreement with Video Games Europe to comply with this Code". Ministry: "den frivillige merkeordningen PEGI", "bransjestyrt", no reason "å gi PEGI-ordningen en formell forankring i norsk lov". Medietilsynet: "tilrådde". Sources: Code; https://stortinget.no/no/Saker-og-publikasjoner/Publikasjoner/Innstillinger/Stortinget/2014-2015/inns-201415-065/1/ (Innst. 65 L 2014-2015); https://www.medietilsynet.no/nyheter/nyhetsarkiv/aktuelt-2020/no-blir-det-hogare-aldersgrense-for-dataspel-som-larer-bort-eller-glamoriserer-gambling/ (2020, extract) | high

## Q4. Markedsføringsloven
- C4.1 §§ 19-21 tied to commercial conduct. § 19: "Når en handelspraksis rettes mot barn, eller for øvrig kan ses eller høres av barn". § 20: "er særskilt rettet mot barn". § 21: "Når markedsføring overfor barn vurderes"; § 21 d: "aggressive virkemidler som vold, seksualitet eller rusmidler". https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/2009-01-09-2 | Lovdata | last amended LOV-2026-05-07-17, in force 01.07.2026 | high
- C4.2 § 1: "markedsføring, handelspraksis og avtalevilkår i forbrukerforhold". § 5 b: "en fysisk eller juridisk person som utøver næringsvirksomhet". § 5 d: "direkte knyttet til å fremme salget av, selge eller levere en ytelse til forbrukerne". | high
- C4.3 No marketing and no trader: sections should not apply (inference). "Levere en ytelse" means free alone doesn't exit § 5 d; deciding question is "næringsdrivende". A sponsor, company or real brand names could change this. | medium
- C4.4 Forbrukertilsynet guidance not read. Gap.
