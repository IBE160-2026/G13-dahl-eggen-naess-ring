# Activities, youth spending, mobile plan and screen repair

Researched on 2026-11-12 for OQ-3 and OQ-17. This covers the activity catalogue (basic and invitation activities), SIFO's reference budget for ages 15-19, the youth mobile plan (current game value NOK 299, unsourced) and phone screen repair (current game value NOK 2,500, unsourced).

Anything marked **[ASSUMPTION]** is an estimate with no direct source. The reasoning is given next to it. "Seen" means the date the page was read. Prices are in NOK and include VAT.

## 1. Proposed activity catalogue

"Game price" is the value we propose for the game: a rounded figure for one person, one occasion, unless the row says otherwise. Ticket fees are included where the source includes them. Transport to the venue is not included.

## 2. Youth everyday spending: SIFO reference budget 2026

Source: **SIFO-rapport 9-2026, "SIFOs referansebudsjett for forbruksutgifter 2026"** (Alecu and Ansteensen, OsloMet), published 2026-05-18, and its Excel file `Referansebudsjettet_2026_Norsk.xlsx` (sheet "Individspes. utgifter 2026"). Handle: https://hdl.handle.net/11250/5517284. February 2026 prices, NOK per month. Seen 2026-11-12. Confidence: **High** (official reference budget).

| Item | Girl 15-17 | Boy 15-17 | Woman 18-24 (food) / >17 | Man 18-24 (food) / >17 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Food and drink (mat og drikke) | 4,120 | 4,640 | 4,445 | 4,945 |
| Clothes and shoes (klær og sko), age 14-17 / >17 | 1,030 | 1,010 | 1,090 | 1,120 |
| Personal care (personlig pleie), age 14-17 / 18+ | 630 | 510 | 1,020 | 820 |
| Leisure, play and media, incl. communication (lek og mediebruk), age 14-17 / >17 | 1,690 | 1,690 | 1,080 | 1,080 |
| Public transport (Ruter 30-day ticket, Oslo, Feb 2026), age 6-19 | 338 | 338 | 338 (age 19) / 1,019 (20+) / 612 (student 20-29) | same |
| **Sum of individual items** | **7,808** | **8,188** | **7,973** (age 18-19) | **8,303** (age 18-19) |

Household items (only if the player lives alone): "mediebruk og fritid" for a one-person household is NOK 2,570 a month (internet, TV/streaming, game console, home insurance and similar); "andre dagligvarer" NOK 400; household articles NOK 590; furniture NOK 550.

Notes:

- SIFO describes "lek og mediebruk" as covering "fritidsutstyr, leker, sikkerhetsutstyr og fritidsaktiviteter, samt medier, kommunikasjon og underholdning", including organised and unorganised leisure activities and social events such as birthday parties. The mobile phone and subscription therefore sit inside this line and are not listed separately. The game should not count the mobile plan on top of the full SIFO leisure figure.
- For a 15-17-year-old living with parents, food is normally covered by the household. For the game, the useful pocket-money lines are clothes (about 1,000), personal care (500-650) and leisure/media (1,690).
- The 1,690 leisure figure is the whole month's need for leisure equipment, activities and media. It gives a ceiling for activity spending: one gym month (349) + cinema (150) + mobile (279) + bowling (260) ≈ 1,040.
- SIFO example: a family of mother, father and a 15-year-old daughter costs NOK 33,456 a month in February 2026 (+939 from February 2025).

## 5. Details and caveats

- **SIFO files.** The report PDF and Excel were downloaded from NVA (Sikt) through the publication's file links. The figures in section 2 are copied from the Excel sheet; the PDF (tables 3-1/3-2 and section 2) gives the same numbers and the category descriptions. SIFO notes that the prices were collected before the closing of the Strait of Hormuz, so later energy-driven price rises are not included.
- **Cinema.** Nordisk Film Kino and Odeon publish no fixed ticket price; the price depends on film, hall and format. No general youth discount was found at NF Kino or Odeon, only student/senior screenings at 120 and KinoPluss (-10). The Film & Kino yearbook 2025 gives a national average ticket price of about NOK 143 (seen only in a search excerpt, so not used as the main source).
- **Festival age limits.** Øya has under-18 and under-23 passes (U/18 was sold out in 2026; no U/18 price found). Tons of Rock allows entry from age 3 (camping and alcohol 18+). Pstereo (Trondheim) has an 18-year age limit, so it does not suit 15-17-year-olds.
- **Gym and age.** Fresh Fitness: from 15, and under 18s cannot buy a bound membership. EVO: a guardian must sign for under-18s (ung.no / Forbrukerrådet). SATS: 15-17 may train alone with a 20% discount; under-18s need a guardian to sign.
- **Football.** Single-match prices differ a lot by club, and many clubs sell mainly season tickets. The Sandefjord youth price (200) is the only official single-match youth price found. Coop/OBOS member deals give 99-110.
- **Tusenfryd** has dynamic prices; 409 is the online "from" price for 2026.
- **The Gathering** price comes from a resale ad stating the ordinary price, so it needs checking against the ticket shop (geekevents) before it goes into the game.
- **Not found with a first-hand source:** official Øya U/18 price, Megazone laser tag price, Samsung Norway repair prices, Peppes official menu, Espresso House Norwegian menu, an official Rockefeller/John Dee price list. These are marked [ASSUMPTION] or low confidence above.
