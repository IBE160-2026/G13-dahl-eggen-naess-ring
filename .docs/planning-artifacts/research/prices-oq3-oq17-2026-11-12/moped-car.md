## 1. Moped insurance

**Proposal: keep NOK 3,000 a year (delkasko, rider aged 16-17).** If the game offers a choice of cover: ansvar NOK 1,800, delkasko NOK 3,000, kasko NOK 5,000 [ASSUMPTION, scaled from the "from" prices below with an extra charge for age 16-17].

No Norwegian insurer publishes a moped price on its own pages. Gjensidige, DNB and Frende all send you to a price calculator, which needs personal details and cannot be read automatically. The figures therefore come from secondary sources that agree with each other:

- **insurio.no, "Mopedforsikring pris 2026"**, seen 2026-11-12 (no publication date; © 2026). "From" prices per year, "based on averages from Gjensidige, If, Tryg and Fremtind":

  | Type | Ansvar | Delkasko | Kasko |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Moped class B (25 km/h) | from 500 | from 1,200 | from 2,000 |
  | Moped class A (45 km/h) | from 700 | from 1,500 | from 2,800 |
  | Scooter / light MC (<125cc) | from 900 | from 1,800 | from 3,500 |
  | Young rider under 25 | from 1,200 | from 2,500 | from 4,500 |

  A 16-year-old is at the very bottom of the under-25 group, so a real price will be above the "from" price.
- **Førerkortet.no, "Hvor mye koster moped lappen?"**, last updated 2026-05-09: moped insurance generally NOK 1,500-5,000 a year, and "often around NOK 2,000-3,000 a year for a young rider". https://www.førerkortet.no/guider/moped-lappen-pris
- **Frende, mopedforsikring**, seen 2026-11-12: "Only people who are of age can buy insurance for a moped. If the moped is registered to an owner under 18, it must be insured under the parents' agreement." Deductible NOK 2,000-4,000. https://www.frende.no/forsikringer/mopedforsikring/ This confirms the rule the earlier research could not confirm.
- **Gjensidige, mopedforsikring**, seen 2026-11-12: the price depends on the moped type, cover, where you live and the rider's age. You must state whether anyone under 23 will ride, otherwise the deductible goes up. Cover levels are ansvar (required by law), delkasko and kasko. https://www.gjensidige.no/forsikring/kjoretoy/mopedforsikring
- **Skatteetaten, trafikkforsikringsavgift**, seen 2026-11-12: the tax for mopeds is NOK 0.39 a day (about NOK 142 a year) from 1 March 2026. It is included in the premium. https://www.skatteetaten.no/satser/trafikkforsikringsavgift/

What the game can teach: it is the parent who holds the policy, the rider's age raises the price, and the premium is often split into monthly payments.

**Still to check:** a real quote for a 16-year-old in a calculator (Gjensidige, If, Tryg or Frende). That needs a person to fill in the form.

## 2. Moped service and repairs

**Proposal: an annual service of NOK 2,900 as a fixed cost, plus random repair events of NOK 500-3,000** [ASSUMPTION for the events: tyre, brake pads, battery or a carburettor clean are typical small jobs]. A cheaper option is "service it yourself or at a friend's" for about NOK 1,500 a year.

- **MC Service AS (Skedsmokorset), verksted page**, seen 2026-11-12, prices for the 2026/2027 season: "Annual service … about NOK 5,500 (moped and scooter normally about NOK 2,900)". Winter storage for mopeds costs NOK 2,490 a season. https://www.mcservice.as/pages/verksted
- **Førerkortet.no**, updated 2026-05-09: "Expect around NOK 1,000-2,000 a year for service, oil and other consumables." No figure for repairs.
- Swedish workshops with fixed prices (for comparison only, in SEK, seen 2026-11-12): Motorsport.se 4-stroke service SEK 1,595; Ingus Vespaservice 2-stroke SEK 1,300 and 4-stroke SEK 1,900; Mölnlycke Mopedcenter SEK 1,995-2,495 without brakes. That is the same level as the Norwegian NOK 2,900.

Only one Norwegian workshop list was found, so the figure rests on a single workshop.

## 3. Used moped, 7-8 years old

**Proposal: NOK 11,000 asking price, NOK 11,645 with the transfer fee.** That replaces NOK 13,750, which was based on two Yamaha Aerox ads.

Source: FINN, search for scooters up to 50cc, model years 2017-2020, 75 hits, read on 2026-11-12. https://www.finn.no/mobility/search/mc/scooter?year_from=2017&year_to=2020&engine_volume_to=50

Removed: an ATV, an electric moped, and two ads at NOK 646 and NOK 1,245 that are clearly not real prices. Mopeds with gears, supermoto and enduro (Derbi Senda, Sherco, Beta, Aprilia RX/SX, Skyteam) are counted under "all", not under "scooters".

| Selection | Number | Median (NOK) | Middle half (NOK) |
|---|---|---|---|
| All valid ads, 2017-2020 | 71 | 12,000 | 7,645-16,645 |
| Scooters, 2017-2020 | 62 | 11,645 | 7,434-16,645 |
| **Scooters, 2018-2019 (7-8 years old)** | **31** | **11,645** | **8,145-17,645** |
| Scooters 2017-2020 without Vespa | 45 | 8,645 | 6,622-12,645 |
| Peugeot Kisbee only, 2017-2020 | 14 | 9,645 | 7,795-13,020 |
| Vespa Primavera/Sprint only | 17 | 19,645 | 17,645-21,822 |

Notes:

- Most prices end in 645 or 145. Skatteetaten's transfer fee (omregistreringsavgift) for mopeds is NOK 645 in 2026 (https://www.skatteetaten.no/satser/omregistreringsavgift/, seen 2026-11-12). This suggests that FINN shows the total price including the fee. It could not be checked in a single ad, because FINN blocks automated reads of ads. The proposal therefore splits the price into NOK 11,000 + NOK 645.
- These are asking prices, not sale prices. Final prices are often somewhat lower.
- Vespa raises the median. A teenager with a tight budget will more likely buy a Kisbee, Neo's or Agility for NOK 8,000-10,000. The game can use NOK 11,000 as the normal price and NOK 8,000 as a "cheap but older" option.
- **Resale value (bonus for OQ-8, low confidence):** a new Kisbee costs NOK 29,000 (earlier research). 2020 Kisbees (6 years old) are listed at about NOK 12,000-14,600, and 2017 models at about NOK 4,100-12,600. That works out to about 12-15 % loss of value a year. It is one model and a small sample.

## 4. Fuel use and distance

### Moped

**3.0 l per 100 km.** Manufacturer figures are lower than real-world use:

- Peugeot Kisbee 50 4T: 1.75 l per 100 km (1000ps.com and the manufacturer's figure cited by motofichas.com); 1.77 l per 100 km (motorcycledb.com, bikez). Seen 2026-11-12.
- Vespa Primavera 50 4T: 2.5 l per 100 km in spec databases (motorcycledb.com, 1000ps.com). Seen 2026-11-12.
- Owner logs on spritmonitor.de, seen 2026-11-12: Kisbee 3.58 l per 100 km over about 7,800 km; Vespa Primavera 50 2017 3.01 l per 100 km, 2018 2.95 l per 100 km, 2019 3.31 l per 100 km over 14,476 km.
- Older 2-strokes can use 4 l per 100 km or more (scooter.co.uk and other secondary sources).

**Distance: 300 km a month in the riding season, 9 l a month, NOK 186 at NOK 20.64** [ASSUMPTION]. Reasoning: 5 km each way to school or work × 20 days = 200 km, plus about 100 km for leisure. The season is set to April-October, because many people put the moped away for the winter (MC Service AS offers winter storage, see item 2). The figure lies between two sources:

- **Upper limit:** TØI report 2138/2026 (Bjørnskau, "Risiko for 16-17-åringer på lett mc …"): 16-17-year-olds with a light MC licence ride on average **5,836 km a year** (about 490 km a month), from a nationwide survey by Statens vegvesen. A light MC is faster than a moped and is used for longer trips. https://www.toi.no/getfile.php/1380489-1772016682/Publikasjoner/T%C3%98I%20rapporter/2026/2138-2026/2138-2026_Sammendrag.pdf
- **Lower limit:** the odometer readings in 55 of the FINN ads above give a median of **about 1,050 km a year** over the moped's life (all owners, all months).

If the game has no seasons: 2,100 km a year / 12 = 175 km a month, 5.25 l = NOK 108 a month.

### Car (driver aged 18-19)

**6.0 l per 100 km** for a small used petrol car, about 10 years old (for example a VW Polo or Toyota Yaris):

- Statens vegvesen, "Energipris per 100 km", updated 2026-08-17: new compact petrol cars 5.20 l per 100 km, the mid-size class 7.67, mid-size SUVs 6.50 (the three best-selling models per segment). https://www.vegvesen.no/nn/koyretoy/eige-og-vedlikehalde/kjoretoy-og-drivstoff/energipris-per-100-km/
- Spritmonitor.de, owner logs for VW Polo 2012-2016 with an ordinary petrol engine: 5.41-6.28 l per 100 km. Seen 2026-11-12. An older car uses somewhat more than a new one, so 6.0 is a reasonable figure.

**800 km a month (9,600 km a year), 48 l a month = NOK 991 a month** [ASSUMPTION]. Reasoning from these figures:

- SSB, Kjørelengder (statistics on driving distance), published 2026-04-08, tables 12577 and 12575 read through the SSB API on 2026-11-12. Average per passenger car in 2025:

  | Group | km a year | km a month |
  |---|---|---|
  | All passenger cars | 11,325 | 944 |
  | Petrol cars | 7,628 | 636 |
  | Cars aged 10-14 years | 10,979 | 915 |
  | Cars aged 15-19 years | 9,279 | 773 |

  SSB breaks the figures down by the car's age, not the owner's age. https://www.ssb.no/transport-og-reiseliv/statistikker/klreg
- TØI 2138/2026: 18-19-year-old drivers drove **838 million km a year** in total (from the national travel survey RVU, average 2021-2024). Divided by the 131,429 people aged 18-19 in 2024 (SSB table 07459), that is about **6,400 km a year for each person in the age group**, including those who have no licence or car. Someone who owns a car will drive more than that, so 9,600 km a year fits.

## 5. Class B: minimum age

- **Licence: 18.** "You must be at least 18 years old to get a Category B driving licence." Statens vegvesen, seen 2026-11-12. https://www.vegvesen.no/en/driving-licences/driving-licence-holders/what-are-you-entitled-to-drive/passenger-car-category-b/
- **Practice driving: 16.** "You must be at least 16 years old." Learners under 25 must have completed the basic traffic course. The supervisor must be at least 25 and have held a licence for the category continuously for the last five years. Statens vegvesen, seen 2026-11-12. https://www.vegvesen.no/en/driving-licences/driver-training/driving-practice/driving-practice-in-the-different-driving-licence-categories/passenger-car-b/
- The theory test can be taken at 17.5 at the earliest (six months before the age requirement). Statens vegvesen, theory test page.
- **A later change, not in force yet:** from **26 November 2028** you can get a class B licence at 17, with accompanied driving until you turn 18 (Statens vegvesen, "Ledsaget kjøring"). https://www.vegvesen.no/forerkort/har-forerkort/endringer-i-forerkortreglene-i-norge/ledsaget-kjoring/ The game can use 18 and 16 as of today.

## 6. Car insurance, age 18-19 (for later)

**Proposal: NOK 15,000 a year (NOK 1,250 a month), range NOK 10,000-25,000** [ASSUMPTION]. No Norwegian source gives a price for an 18-19-year-old.

- The average for all private car owners is about **NOK 10,300 a year**, after a price rise of 16.6 % over twelve months, according to Finans Norge and SSB, as cited by bytt.no ("Bilforsikring: så mye koster en bilforsikring i 2026"), seen in search results on 2026-11-12. bytt.no blocks automated reads, so the article was not read in full. Another bytt.no article gives NOK 15,600, so the sources do not agree.
- Young drivers pay more. Gjensidige, If and Fremtind apply a young-driver supplement until age 23, and the deductible goes up by NOK 10,000-15,000 if an undeclared driver under 23 causes a crash (bytt.no, seen 2026-11-12). A newly qualified driver has no bonus.
- The traffic insurance tax for cars is **NOK 6.52 a day** (about NOK 2,380 a year) from 1 March 2026 (Skatteetaten, seen 2026-11-12). It is included in the premium and sets a floor.
- For comparison (Denmark, DKK, 2026): an 18-year-old with a small car pays about DKK 7,000-10,000 for liability plus partial casco (tjekforsikringer.dk), and Gjensidige Denmark's average for ages 18-24 is DKK 14,864.

A Norwegian 18-year-old on delkasko with no bonus is therefore estimated at about 1.5 times the average for all ages: NOK 15,000. Check this in an insurer's calculator before the figure goes into the game.
