- (assumption) Round 3, wellbeing: the team asked whether the barometer is felt in play or is only static. High stress has no in-play mechanical effect beyond these. The draft makes the existing consequences visible (mood, 'snitt så langt' vs threshold, slot loss); a new in-play stress rule is a PRD question for the team.
