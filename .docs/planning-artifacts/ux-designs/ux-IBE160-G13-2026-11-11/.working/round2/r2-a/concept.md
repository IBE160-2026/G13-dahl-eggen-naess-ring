# r2-a – Blits

**The idea.** On the 1st of every month, the room on `#hjem` is "shot" with a flash. It sits in a white instant-photo frame with a felt-tip caption ("November · 16 år"), and over the year the photos build up into a wall. Around it sits a plain, modern web app: a product first, a memory wall second.

**Signature move.** The **timeline bar is a photo-booth strip** of 12 named months. Past months show the room as it looked then (snow in January, the moped from April). Now is outlined; the future is blank film. The **echo badge is a pinned photo** ("Januar · 15") beside "Fra januar: pengegaven". `#aar` lays out the year's photos, a five-segment age rail and the unlocks still to come.

**Game dynamics.** "Fortsett" fires the flash: a white veil fades and the photo develops. It is the one authored motion, off under reduced motion. Changed values keep a yellow "Ny" tag until seen. The month plan is tap-to-place with a live total, money left and wellbeing (68 → 74, as in worked example 26a). "Hvorfor?" expands in place with the sum, Åpnet/Stengt says what moved, and the result photo shows the change in the room.

**Visual language.** A cool grey wall, white photo paper, ink text and slate chrome. Flash yellow is used only for the primary action, "now" and "new". The photos are flash-lit: cyan shadows, hard flash shadows, a vignette. Hierarchy comes from scale: 48–64 px semi-expanded Archivo figures with tabular numbers against 13 px labels. The marker face is used only on photo captions.

**Desktop and tablet.** A wall: the large photo with docked panes for status and Fortsett, money, wellbeing, phone and the month plan. `#telefon` becomes a list plus a reader, and `#plan` and `#penger` become multi-pane. From 768 px a slate rail replaces the bottom bar.

**Where it sits.** Fresher than Ly/Rommet (no warmth). Calmer than Månedsfeed (light ground, one accent, no reaction bursts).

**Risks.** The strip labels are 11 px condensed so they fit at 320 px. The thumbnails need real per-month art. Yellow must stay rare, or "Ny" loses its meaning. Fonts must be self-hosted (NFR-3).

**Invented figures** (not in Rule Set v1):
- the 1 250 kr split option (2 500 kr is the Rule Set figure)
- the stress effects of the event options
- the effects of the hyttetur and gaming activities
- the Småtteri amounts (600/450/240 kr)
- the August and October entries in the year log
