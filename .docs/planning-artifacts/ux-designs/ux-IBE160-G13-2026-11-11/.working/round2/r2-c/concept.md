# r2-c – Varsel

**The idea.** LifeMode borrows the data graphic every Norwegian teen reads daily: the weather app's meteogram. The room is the "now" panel, and its window shows the month's weather and light (November sleet, December snow).

**Signature move: life as a forecast.** The timeline bar is a meteogram strip of the 12 named months. Past months are filled and carry a thin wellbeing line, the current month is outlined, and future months stay empty, because events are never forecast. The year view is the meteogram expanded, with month symbols, what happened, and the age rail 15–19. "Neste måned" in the money view is a forecast card of known bills: "LifeMode varsler aldri hendelser."

**Game dynamics.** A quiet month is one tap. **Fortsett til desember** fades the window to December, extends the line by one column, and opens the new values as *held changes* with a «Ny» marker that stays until the player opens them. The plan works by tapping: pick a slot, then «Legg til». Slot 4 is hatched as «Tatt av ekstravakter», and the totals and wellbeing-with-plan update live. A highlight event dims the room and spotlights the cracked phone. The result returns to the room ("Mobilen er hel igjen") with worded deltas, an aurora-coloured **echo badge** (the pengegave covered NOK 136), «Hvorfor?» expanding in place and Åpnet/Stengt.

**Visual language.** Cool slate, fjord blue and crisp white. Data colours are used only where they mean something: teal for wellbeing, yellow for an event, red for money out, aurora for echoes, blue for held changes. Type is Geist only, with tabular figures and hierarchy from scale and weight. Data sits in ruled tables. The one authored motion is the month opening, and everything respects reduced motion.

**Desktop and tablet.** A weather dashboard: a fjord-blue rail, the strip as the header, the room plus this month as the big now-panel, and Mobil, Penger, Neste måned and Plan as data panes.

**Where it sits.** Månedsfeed's realism and crispness without the neon, and Ly/Rommet's calm with cool daylight instead of lamplight.

**Risks.** It can read as a utility app. The room, the season light and the month opening carry the play, and need playtesting.

**Invented figures.** These are invented: the year 2027, the Jan–Oct wellbeing values and year log, kino and trening, the hyttetur, the screen options other than the NOK 2,500 repair, the event's stress effects, and the Småtteri totals. «Mangler 1 236 kr» is a shown state only, because under FR-45 savings would cover it. December's figures are my own arithmetic from Rule Set v1. The nickname conflicts with the PRD. Geist must be self-hosted (NFR-3), and the plan totals are computed client-side for the demo only (NFR-4).
