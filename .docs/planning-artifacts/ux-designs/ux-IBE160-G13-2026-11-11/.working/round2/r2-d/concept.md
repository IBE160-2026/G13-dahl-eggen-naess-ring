# r2-d – Stue (the Nordic interior lookbook)

## The idea
Mak's room is drawn like a Scandinavian interior lookbook page, straight-on in soft November daylight, with product tags on the objects that work as the menu. It is the muted end, kept modern by a cool palette, one matte cobalt accent and strict type.

## Signature move
**Hotspot tags.** Each object carries a dot and a tag: `TELEFON 2 nye`, `PLAN 3 ledige`, `PENGER 2 364 kr`, `JOBB 14 t`. A filled cobalt dot means new and stays until seen. Belongings get tags too (the moped outside since June), and `Ledig plass` tags show 3 of 6 things. An open system's tag turns ink.

## Game dynamics
- The hub leads with November; `Fortsett til desember` is pinned, one tap.
- **Held change:** «Nytt siden oktober», room dots, phone badge and money chips stay marked until you leave the screen where you saw them.
- **Event:** the room desaturates; only the moped tag stays, in lingon. Options are actions with kroner; wellbeing is hidden until the pick.
- **Result:** signed, worded deltas, echo badge, «Hvorfor?» in place, Åpnet/Stengt; the room tag becomes `fikset`.
- **Plan:** tap to place; a hatched fourth slot; live cost and trivsel (68 → 74, example 26).

## Visual language
Matte fog, stone, sage, birch and ink-blue. Cobalt only for action, new and now; lingon only for missing money. One family, **Albert Sans**, four sizes (12/15/22/40); weight and caps carry rank. Small radii. One motion: tags settling as the month opens (reduced-motion safe). Fonts self-hosted in production (NFR-3).

## Desktop and tablet
≥1000 px is a lookbook spread: rail, large room, and the opened system as a sliding side panel, so the room stays visible. At 720–999 px the room sits above a three-column hub; money uses two columns.

## Where it sits
Ly/Rommet in mood, Månedsfeed in discipline: realistic bank and phone patterns, dense panes, crisp tags.

## Risks
Muted can feel low-energy for 15-year-olds; the room and change moments must carry the delight. Tags can collide at 320 px. Illustration cost grows with 6 layers × states.

## Invented figures
The October moped event (1 800 kr repair within the assumed 500–3 000 range, 400 kr DIY, all its wellbeing effects); 2 300 kr before it (back-calculated from example 25); the year history, earlier Småtteri, kino/festival effects and belongings. «Mangler» counts brukskonto only to show the state (FR-45 also counts savings).
