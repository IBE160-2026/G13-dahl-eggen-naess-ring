# r2-b – Ringer

## The idea
LifeMode in the grammar of messages, stories and banking apps. The room is the hub, a crisp flat illustration with personality (a yellow hoodie, a moped in the snow). The phone and bank feel like real apps.

## Signature move
**Story rings on room objects.** Mobil (with a count), Plan and Penger get a Kobolt ring when their system has changed. Opening the system settles it to a grey "seen" ring. Jobb has no news, so no ring. Deltas arrive as reaction chips ("+8 457 kr lønn inn"). The timeline is 12 story ticks, jan–des.

## Game dynamics
- **Month loop:** November opens with three rings. A quiet month is one tap on "Fortsett til desember".
- **Held change:** chips keep a blue "new" dot, and changed tiles keep an outline, until you open that system.
- **Plan:** tap to place, never drag. "Legg i månedsplanen" in Emma's message fills the plan live.
- **Event → result:** the room dims to a spotlight on the cracked phone, and the options show only kroner. The result has reaction chips, an echo card naming last year's pengegave choice, an inline "Hvorfor?" and Åpnet/Stengt.

## Visual language
White surfaces on a cool grey canvas for daylight on the bus, with a true dark mode for bed at night. There is one accent, **Kobolt #2645FF**, for actions, rings and "now". The state colours: green for in, red for out, amber for "Mangler", magenta for echo. The type is **Figtree** from 400 to 900, with tabular figures. Everything sits on a strict 4/8 px grid. Motion is just the ring settle and the chip pop.

## Desktop and tablet
The desktop works like a messaging app: a rail, the **room permanently in the centre**, and a right pane with the system you opened. Plan, Year and Event take the centre. The tablet layout is a rail, the room, a status row and an inbox preview.

## Where it sits
At the fresh end, beside Månedsfeed, but with white instead of black, Kobolt instead of lime, and a room instead of a reel.

## Risks
It is the most familiar look, so the room must carry the game feel. Figtree will be self-hosted (NFR-3).
