# Kortstokk (c7)

**Idea.** LifeMode as a roguelike deckbuilder: each month is a turn, free time is your energy (1–4 slots), school and work are locked cards on the board, and everything entering your life arrives as a card. A run is 60 turns, 15 to 20.

## Game dynamics
- **Turn:** the hub ("brettet") shows a 60-segment run meter, balance, trivsel 58/100 with three numbered parts, the room as player card, and four decks (Telefon, Månedsplan, Jobb, Penger). "Avslutt mars" ends the turn; a quiet month is one tap.
- **The phone deals your hand:** Emma's invitation becomes a "Konsert med Emma" card valid this month only. Tap card, tap slot; no drag.
- **Slots are mana:** extra shifts lock the fourth token. Cards show kr cost and STRESS/SOSIALT stats with sign and word; unaffordable cards are hatched with "MANGLER 430 kr".
- **Events are boss cards** with A/B/C action cards (one GRATIS). On the year map future bosses lie face down: you know *when*, not *what*. Krisekort and kortvarsel are wild cards that can be shuffled in.
- **Oppgjør:** results resolve as a tally, balance before → after, a "SPILT" stamp, and ÅPNET/LUKKET cards.
- **Echo badges are chain links** drawn back to the causing card ("Ekko fra mnd 1: gaming-PC fra pengegaven"); **Hvorfor?** is one tap away.
- **Belongings are relics:** gold cards with ongoing effects ("+7 kr/mnd rente", "−249 kr/mnd"). Locked relics wait on the board, and open offers preview their running cost ("Moped etterpå: −1 245 kr/mnd"), making months-ahead thinking concrete.

## Visual language
Dark felt table, cream card stock, chunky ink frames, soft shadows. Rarity colour always paired with shape and label: Hendelse red hexagon, Invitasjon mint diamond, Tilbud cobalt triangle, Eiendel gold star, Krise pink octagon, Låst hatching + padlock. Archivo Black for numbers/titles, Archivo Narrow for card text. Original flat vector art. Motion: cards deal in, snap into slots, tally lines count up.

## Why it works for 15–20
Card games are a grammar they already read; cost, stats and lasting effects make money literacy feel like a run, not a budget.

## Risks
The metaphor can make money abstract (kroner stay prominent); card density at 320 px; "lower stress is better" must be taught early.

## Prototype first
Month plan tap-card/tap-slot with running total, then the event tally with echo chain.
