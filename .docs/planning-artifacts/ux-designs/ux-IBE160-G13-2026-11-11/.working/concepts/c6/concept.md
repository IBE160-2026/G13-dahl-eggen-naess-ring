# c6 – Rommet (cozy isometric diorama)

**The idea.** The player's bedroom is the whole interface. An isometric diorama fills the hub, and each system lives in an object: the phone on the bed opens messages, the wall calendar opens the year and the month plan, the piggy bank opens the money "kassabok", and the work apron opens the job view. The window shows the month and the street, where the moped is parked once you own one. Belongings collect over five years, so the room becomes a portrait of the life you lived.

**Game dynamics.** A month opens with the room reacting: the phone glows with new messages, and the calendar flips. A quiet month is one "Fortsett til april" tap. An active month is a *scene*: the room dims to a spotlight on the object involved (a cracked phone), and the sender speaks in a bubble. The options are actions with signed kroner and wellbeing effects, and one is always free. The result returns to the room: the phone is whole again, the deltas show as worded chips, an echo badge ("Ekko fra måned 1: pengegaven") links this month's interest to an old choice, "Hvorfor?" expands in place, and "Lukket/Åpent" shows what moved. The month plan is sticky notes on the calendar page: school and work are locked, hatched blocks, and you tap an activity into a free slot. Activities you can't afford show "Mangler 1 010 kr". Stress makes the room messier and dimmer, but wellbeing and its three parts are always written as numbers too.

**Visual language.** A warm Scandinavian interior: oat walls, pine-green panelling, a birch floor, a rust rug and ochre lamplight. The landing is a night scene with the room glowing. Type is Fraunces (warm display) plus Atkinson Hyperlegible (legible numbers on a bus). The isometric projection is real, with wall items skewed onto the walls.

**Why it works for 15–20.** It's their own space, not a bank. Filling the room is a cozy-game reward loop.

**Risks.** Discoverability depends on the labels, so every object has a permanent text tag and a menu route stays. Illustration cost grows with 6 layers × states. The room must stay readable at 320 px.

**Prototype first.** The labelled hub room and the event scene → result flow, to test whether players find the phone and calendar unaided.
