# c5 · Riso-zine

**Idea.** LifeMode is a risograph zine a Norwegian 17-year-old prints about their own life: one issue per month, 60 in all. It uses off-white paper, fluor pink and riso blue printed off-register over black, halftones, torn scraps, tape, stamps and margin scribbles. The page number is the month ("s. 15").

## Game dynamics
- **The loop is a page turn.** Each month opens as a new spread with the month name huge, a sticker reading "måned 15 av 60", the room collage, a balance receipt and a wellbeing stamp. A quiet month gets a scribble ("ett trykk, så er du i april") and one torn ticket: *Fortsett til april*.
- **Choices are stickers you tap down.** Options state "Koster 1 290 kr · stress −1". The free option is taped "GRATIS VALG". An activity you can't afford is striped and locked: "mangler 342 kr".
- **Consequences are receipts.** A result prints before → after values with a BETALT stamp. The margin talks back: "← sparte 441 kr på forsikring. betalte 1 290 nå. hmm."
- **Echo badges are taped-in scraps** of an earlier page ("s. 8: nei til forsikring"), so carrying a choice forward becomes something you can see.
- **Hvorfor? is a fold-out note** with numbered causes. *Åpnet* and *Lukket seg litt* sit underneath it.
- **The room** is cut-paper layers (4 of 6), each with a typed caption.

## Visual language
Three inks overprinting with multiply. Big Shoulders Display is the ransom-note headline face. Courier Prime is the typewriter face for all body text and numbers, and its monospace keeps figures aligned. Permanent Marker is for scribbles only. Motion: stickers slap down with a small overshoot, and a new month arrives as a page turn.

## Why it works for 15–20
It draws on DIY, collage and sticker culture, not school or bank visuals. Money shown as receipts is money teens already read.

## Risks
Texture can turn into noise, so body text sits on plain paper and only display type misregisters. Fluor pink is never used as text; pink text is #B3125F. Rotation on interactive elements stays at 1–2° or less. Filters and blend modes need a performance check on cheap phones.

## Prototype first
The 03b sticker month plan (tap slot, tap sticker) and the 04b result receipt with the taped echo and the Hvorfor? fold-out.
