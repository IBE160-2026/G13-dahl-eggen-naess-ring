# c4 — Mobilen (Ly)

**The idea.** The whole game is the character's own phone. It runs a made-up OS called **Ly** (Norwegian for shelter). Its emblem is a selburose built from eight petals. The landing screen is the phone being set up: the rose is only half built, and notifications from the life ahead ("Emma: Konsert?", "Minner: For 1 år siden…", "Hege ringer …") drift over it. Then *Du er 15. Ny mobil. Fem år foran deg.* and Opprett bruker / Logg inn.

**Game dynamics.**
- *The month* is the home screen. The big clock reads **Mars** instead of a time. A thin **livslinja** across the top of every screen fills as the 60 months pass. Widgets show Trivsel (number plus three bars), Penger and Rommet. **Fortsett til april** sits where swipe-to-unlock would be, but one tap is enough.
- *Highlight events* arrive as **incoming calls** from the sender (Hege, the class teacher). Each answer is a big button with a money chip and wellbeing chips. Right above them is the line that creates the tension: "Mopedlappen 100 % spart".
- *Consequences* appear when the call ends: delta cards, Åpnet / Lukket ("Mopedlappen i april – mangler nå 3 192 kr") and the "hvis du hadde stått over" line.
- *Echo badges* are **Minner** notifications, the familiar "for one year ago" photo-memory pattern pointed at your own choices.
- *Hvorfor?* is a dark system sheet ("Ly forklarer") that splits a change into causes and rules.
- *Room*: the **Rull** app is the camera roll of the room, layer by layer. A dashed ghost moped shows a belonging you have not unlocked yet.
- *Year*: Kalender shows the year as a timeline. Highlight months are marked with a rose, unlocks with a key and boomerang follow-ups with the Minner loop. Months that could become a crisis are noted too.
- *Activities*: a friend's invitation goes into the month plan with "Legg i månedsplan".
- *Wallpaper* changes with life stage.

**Visual language.** Night blue and warm snow-white, with Norwegian accents: multe (cloudberry orange) for actions, rogn, fjord, brunost and bjørk for the apps, and gold for Minner. The signature shape is the **lapp**: every icon tile, widget and option has one folded top-right corner. Icons are drawn in a 2.2-stroke line. The savings goal fills as knitted *masker* (stitches). Type is Bricolage Grotesque for display and Schibsted Grotesk (Norwegian-made) for UI. Plus and minus always carry a sign and a word ("−3 500 kr ut", "Sosialt +8"). Motion intent: one moment only, where the rose finishes building as setup completes.

**Why it works for 15–20-year-olds.** The phone is their native interface, so there is nothing new to learn. A call, a notification or a memory feels like life, not schoolwork. The setting also makes the inbox, bank and calendar logical rather than menus.

**Risks.** The OS chrome could crowd the content on small screens. A phone inside the real phone might feel meta or claustrophobic. The look has to stay clearly fictional so it never reads as a real banking app. Dense money screens need care.

**Prototype first.** The incoming-call event, then the result, then Hvorfor? as a clickable flow, together with one-tap Fortsett through a quiet month. That tests whether the call metaphor beats reading.
