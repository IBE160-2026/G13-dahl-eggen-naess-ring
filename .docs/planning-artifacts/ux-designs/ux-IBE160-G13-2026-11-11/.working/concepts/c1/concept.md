# c1 – Månedsfeed (Story-feed)

**The idea.** LifeMode uses the grammar a 15-year-old already reads without thinking: stories, reactions, reposts, DMs, creator stats. Each in-game year is a story reel. The top of every screen has 12 ticks, one per month, and the four highlight months are fat ticks. Every month is one story you tap through. The point is not to parody a feed. It is to make "what did my choice do?" feel as instant as a reaction on a post.

## Game dynamics
- **Monthly loop = a story.** The hub (02) is the month's cover story: your room full-bleed, a caption ("Ingen hendelse. Lønna kom inn."), sticker chips for money in and out, and four highlight bubbles (Telefon, Månedsplan, Jobb, Penger). A ring on a bubble means something new. **Fortsett** is one lime bar, so a quiet month takes one tap.
- **Highlight event = a full-bleed story card** (04a) from a sender (Mamma). The options are action cards with kroner and wellbeing on each one. One option is always free, and one you can't afford shows the missing amount.
- **Consequences = a reaction burst** (04b): stickers for −1 490 kr and Stress −2 pop in and settle. Then comes the **echo**, a repost of your own earlier choice: "Du for 8 måneder siden: Nei takk til mobilforsikring." The same pink repost mark is used everywhere for echo badges, even in the timeline ("kan gi ekko").
- **Hvorfor? = a light bottom sheet** that cuts through the dark feed. It shows a stacked cause bar (500 kr deductible + 990 kr echo) and plain text with the figures.
- **Room = the story background.** New layers appear in it. Empty layers show as dashed outlines ("mopedhjelm?"), so the room also teases what you might own next.
- **Month plan** shows school and work as locked, hatched blocks and free time as tappable slots. An echo chip explains why there are 3 slots instead of 4.

## Visual language
Near-black **Natt** base, **Volt** lime for action, money in and "now", and **Puls** pink for echoes and reactions. Amber is only for missing amounts and warnings. Archivo Black carries big, confident numbers and headlines. Schibsted Grotesk, a Norwegian-made grotesk, is the precise UI voice. Shapes are pills and 20–28 px cards. Every delta has a sign and a word.
**Motion intent:** snappy and haptic. Ticks fill as the month plays. A card advances with a short horizontal push and a tap-thud. Reaction stickers scale from 0.6 to 1.05 to 1 in 220 ms. The echo repost slides up from below, the way a reshared post appears. Nothing depends on gestures: every swipe has a tap equivalent.

## Why it works for 15–20
It needs no tutorial. Ticks, rings and reposts are already understood, and the game borrows feed rhythm instead of school rhythm.

## Risks
Feed grammar can read as "another app wanting my attention", so the copy must stay calm and non-addictive. Dark, saturated colour needs strict contrast discipline. The fat and thin ticks are subtle at small sizes, so they need labels.

## Prototype first
The 04a → 04b moment: event, choice, reaction burst, echo repost, Hvorfor?. Then test whether players link the repost to their earlier choice.
