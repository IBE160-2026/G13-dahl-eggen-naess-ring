# Blokka — Experience Delta

## Foundation

Blokka inherits everything in the shared spine (`../../EXPERIENCE.md`): the 28 surfaces, voice, states, interaction primitives, accessibility floor and key flows. This file holds only the delta: where each surface lives in the block, how the concept's components behave, and how shared states and flows look here. Visual tokens are in `DESIGN.md`. On product rules the shared spine and the PRD win; on metaphor and layout this file wins. Sketches: 28 SVG screens generated by `alle.py` in the Fjord palette.

## Metaphor Map

Your life is one apartment block: street level («BUTIKKEN», «BANKEN»), five floors = År 1–5 (15–19 år), the life goal as a flag on the roof, a lift tower showing your floor. You move in on the 1st floor at 15 and take the lift up each New Year.

## Component Patterns

Delta only; shared rules apply.

| Component | Blokka behavior |
|---|---|
| Area entry points | The doorbell panel. A plate whose bell is filled carries its waiting count as text; a plate with nothing waiting shows an empty bell and no note. Ringing (tapping) a plate opens that area. |
| Year progress | The month strip is separate from the building (the user rejected months as lamps in the scene). Lit windows in lived floors repeat the same fact in the scene. |
| Locked item | Future floors are lifted above yours and see-through; they cannot be entered. What opens is told in text on the year end (23) and welcome back (24). |
| Result | Each change becomes one note on the wall; notes and the new photo stay on the wall in later months as the room's memory [ASSUMPTION: how long notes stay is not sketched]. |
| "Hvorfor?" | When the cause lies in an earlier year, the echo card shows a mini block with a glowing window in that floor. |
| Next month | Ordinary months: «Neste måned» + next month name. December: the lift ride (23) replaces it, «Ta heisen til År N». |
| Phone | Steps out of the block into a real phone UI; the invitation's «Legg i planen» fills a slot in 15. |

## Interaction Primitives

- One fixed isometric camera. No rotating, pinching or zooming the block; the hub is already zoomed in on your floor.
- Everything tappable in the scene has a text twin in the UI below (doorbells, options, links); the scene itself is never the only way to act.
- The lift moves only as a result of finishing a year, never on its own or on a timer.

## Accessibility

- Every floor has a text alternative: «År 2, 16 år, spilt, 12 av 12 vinduer tent» / «År 3, din etasje» / «År 4, 18 år, låst».
- The open floor is described as a list of what is in it (bed, desk, photo from the trip, moped helmet), changes first.
- Wall notes are read as a list in reading order, matching the result text below them.
- Lit vs dark windows (3.61:1) always have a count or a legend in text («Tente vinduer = måneder du har spilt»); the window meter reads «Trivsel 64 av 100, grensen er 50».
- Month strip cells expose «november, denne måneden», «oktober, spilt», «desember, kommer», plus «hendelse» where the dot is.
- Skewed text on 3D faces («BUTIKKEN», «3. etg») is decorative; the same words exist as real text on the surface.
- Lift ride (23) and confetti (25) are decorative and skipped with reduced motion.

## Key Flows

- **Flow 1 climax (13):** after the first real choice, notes go up on your wall and the amount, wellbeing change and window meter appear below. The room itself has changed.
- **Flow 3 climax (14):** «Hvorfor?» links the change to the earlier choice; the echo card lights a window in the År 1 floor of a mini block: «Du sparte pengegaven. Derfor hadde du råd.»
- **Flow 6 climax (25):** the whole block lights up, the roof flag gets a check, and «Valgene som betydde mest» lists three choices, each tagged with the floor color where it was made.
- Each year end (23) adds a lift ride to the next floor, the concept's rhythm between Flow 1 and Flow 6.

## Inspiration & Anti-patterns

From the memlog, in the order the user rejected them:

- **Rejected: information overload, ugly colors, many elements** (round 1). One main thing per screen; the scene is calm daylight.
- **Rejected: childish/cartoon, school or learning material, a static picture you tap, a bank app as a whole.** Bank elements live only in the money view at street level.
- **Rejected: too similar to Veien** (v1 front cross-section, v2 people and design). Led to the isometric dollhouse.
- **Rejected: light bulbs as a timeline** (v2 ceiling lamps). Months moved to a separate strip.
- **Rejected: block-style 3D figures** (v3). Replaced by soft, slim avatars with natural proportions.
- **Rejected palettes:** the v2 cobalt/tomato and the «solnedgang» and «mint» alternatives; Fjord chosen.
- **Kept from v2:** doorbell panel as the area menu, events in the stairwell, results as notes on the wall, wellbeing as lit windows, one facade color per year.
