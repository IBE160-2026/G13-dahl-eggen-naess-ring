Scope: LifeMode mobile game screens (390x844 SVG concept sketches, 28 screens), visitor mode Experience.
Audience/job: Norwegian players 15-20 play one in-game year per session on a phone; every game screen is one scene, choices are things inside the scene.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The screen is a mirror selfie in the player's own room: a tall mirror leaning on the wall, the reflection holds the phone up with the camera to the glass. Refuses tabs, card stacks and button rows.
OWN-WORLD: Graphic illustration, flat shapes with one shade tone and light grain; sage wall, oak floor, black mirror frame, cobalt hoodie, coral phone case; yellow post-its only for locked things; white pill stickers for status; coral stickers for primary actions; Kalam for handwriting, Bricolage Grotesque for system text.
STORY: The year hangs as a photo garland of twelve clips above the mirror; played months get photos, the current month hangs largest. Progress shows on the reflection and as things in the room (moving boxes toward the life goal).
FIRST VIEWPORT: Garland across the top with "År 2 av 5"; mirror fills the left two thirds; "Du, 16 år" in marker with an arrow to the reflection; locked post-its top right of the glass; mobil, trivsel and penger stickers on the lower frame; work shirt and the light switch (next month) on the wall to the right.
