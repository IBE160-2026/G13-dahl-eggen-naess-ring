# Prose review: LifeMode PRD (polish pass before final)

- **Reviewed:** `prd.md` (updated 2026-11-13), 28,396 words, 1,544 lines (word_metrics.py), after structure rows 1-13, 15, 16, 18-25 and the Tier/Trigger glossary fix were applied
- **Lens:** prose · **Reader type:** humans · **Style guide:** Microsoft Writing Style Guide
- **Reviewed on:** 2026-11-13

**Purpose and audience:** This document exists to help the team, the downstream BMAD work (UX, architecture, epics and stories), and the examiner agree on what LifeMode version one must do and why. It also gives builders and testers stable, testable FR, NFR, SM, and OQ statements to build and check against.

**Structure model (from the structure pass):** Strategic/Context (Pyramid) for §0-§4 and §7-§9, and Reference/Database for §5-§6.

**Style to preserve:** short declarative sentences, contractions ("isn't", "it's"), and second-level bold lead-ins inside bullets. The spelling is British with -ize ("behaviour", "colour", "licence", "judgement", alongside "Realizes", "summarize"). That's a consistent house style, so keep it. Also keep NOK figures written as "NOK 10,000", the Norwegian domain terms (pengegave, BSU, frikort, grunnskole, Vg2, trygdeavgift), the Norwegian UI strings in «» and quotes, and the worked journeys with real kroner figures. None of the rows below changes a figure, an ID, a heading, or a UI string.

**Overall:** the prose is already clean. The rows below cover four things:

- one contradiction about what the tiers mean (row 1)
- pointers left dangling or unclear after the structure fixes (rows 2, 6, 10, 11, 30)
- testable statements whose wording allows two readings (rows 14-16, 24, 25)
- run-on sentences and undefined jargon in otherwise plain text: "thesis", "rows", "step", "one-at-a-time rule", "fit"

Most of the newly added or rewritten text (§0 Related documents, §4.4 order, the §7.3 F table, the NFR-7 groups, NFR-3 **Playtests**, FR-55, FR-48) reads well. The rows below name the few places where it doesn't.
