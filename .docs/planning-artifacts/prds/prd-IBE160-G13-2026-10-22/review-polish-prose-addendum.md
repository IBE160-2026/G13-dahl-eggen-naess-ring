# Prose review: PRD addendum

**Read of purpose:** this document exists to help the team, the downstream architecture and UX work, and the examiner find the detail and the reasons behind the PRD's decisions, without that detail weighing down the PRD itself.

**Word count** (from `word_metrics.py`): 12,910 words in the current file.

**Style to preserve:** a terse decision-record voice; telegraphic fragments in the UJ-4 entry state and in checklist bullets ("Wage 10 h a week, NOK 8,452."); contractions, as Microsoft style allows; Norwegian game and domain terms left unitalicized (pengegave, BSU, utlånsforskriften); ISO dates; "h a week"; the "Decided on …" opening line in each history section; and the italic *Superseded …* notes. None of the rows below changes a figure, an ID, a heading, or a Norwegian UI string.

**Structure lens's three minor fixes:**
- *Run-on in the "Review fixes" intro:* already fixed in the current text ("… left open or contradictory. They are numbered R1-R12 …"). No row.
- *"Labels" bullet in "For UX" repeats P6:* accepted as reinforcement for a different audience. No row.
- *Mixed quote styles for Norwegian UI strings:* checked against the PRD. The PRD puts whole Norwegian sentences and labels in guillemets («Konserter og arrangementer», «Du har nådd halvparten av sparemålet ditt») and the "Hvorfor?" button in straight quotes, 17 times. The addendum follows the same convention, so it is consistent with the PRD. No row. If the team wants a single style, change it in both documents at once.

## Summary

- **Findings:** 40 rows (34 direct fixes, 5 "Consider", 1 QUESTION on a likely stale ID), plus 5 minor fixes rolled into one line.
- **Highest impact:** the stale "(D1)" reference; "before wellbeing" with no verb; "slots" used for active months; the conditional in the summer-job aside that turns into fact; and the run-on of reasons in "Interest after a long break".
- **Length:** net about +30 words if all rows are accepted (mostly the added verbs and definitions). No length target was given.
- **Preserved:** figures, IDs, headings, Norwegian UI strings, and the PRD's quote convention for them. No row cuts an example, a summary, or a table.
