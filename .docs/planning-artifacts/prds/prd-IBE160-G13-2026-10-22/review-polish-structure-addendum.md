# Structure review: PRD addendum

**Read of purpose:** this document exists to help the team, the downstream architecture and UX work, and the examiner find the detail and the reasons behind the PRD's decisions, without that detail weighing down the PRD itself.

**Structure model:** a mix. The first H2, "Detail for downstream work", is reference material that readers jump into, so it's judged against **Reference/Database** (random access, MECE topics, consistent schema). "Decision history" is a set of decision records, judged against **Strategic/Context** (grouping, most critical first). The mismatch is the main structural weakness: the history is ordered by date, and readers come to it by topic ("why is the wellbeing window 19-60?"). Because the IDs and headings must stay stable and the document is about to be final, the recommendations below add navigation and remove misplaced or duplicated text. None of them reorganizes the history.

**Word counts** (from `word_metrics.py`): 12,177 words in total. Detail for downstream work: 3,724 (31%). Changes from the proposal: 266 (2%). Decision history: 7,674 (63%). Confirmed assumptions: 513 (4%).

**Referenced headings and IDs checked:** the PRD cites the addendum headings "Ready for V2" (PRD line 281) and "Changes from the proposal" (OQ-15). The addendum cites "Login with email: decisions, limits, and reset routes", "Playtest data", "UJ-4 month by month", "UJ-4: card warning, family help, moped, and car licence", "The ending screen", "Review fixes", "UX concept alignment", and "Option fields" internally. No GitHub issue body cites an addendum heading. Earlier review files cite headings too, but they are historical records. Only row 15 renames a heading, and it is flagged.

## Summary

- **Recommendations:** 19 rows (15 actionable: 2 MERGE scaffolds, 4 MOVE, 4 CONDENSE, 2 CUT, 1 MERGE of duplicate text, 2 QUESTION; plus 4 PRESERVE).
- **Estimated change if all are accepted:** about −258 words cut (rows 3, 4, 6, 11, 12, 14) and about +185 words added (rows 1, 2, 5, 15), so a net of about −75 words (−0.6% of 12,177). The value is in findability and in the conflict rule working as intended, not in length. No length target was given.
- **Headings and IDs:** no R, D, A, OQ, or UJ ID changes. Only row 15 renames a heading (low risk, flagged). Row 9's alternative would add a heading and change one internal pointer; the preferred option avoids that.
- **Comprehension trade-offs:** none of the cuts removes a summary, example, or visual aid. The two scaffolding additions (rows 2 and 5) make the document slightly longer to help readers find their way.
