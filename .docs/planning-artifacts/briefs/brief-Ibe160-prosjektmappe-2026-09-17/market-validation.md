# Market Validation

## Pre-development User Research

Before development, two separate surveys will be used to validate the underlying problem, user needs and commercial assumptions.

### Player survey

The player survey will explore whether the problem feels relevant, which financial situations are most interesting, whether the concept sounds entertaining rather than like traditional schoolwork, whether mechanics such as Life Balance and persistent consequences make sense, and whether users would be interested in playing such a game.

### Parent survey

A separate parent survey will investigate whether parents see value in giving teenagers a safe environment for practising financial decisions, what functionality they would expect, which concerns they have around privacy and AI, and whether there is willingness to pay for a future Family version.

## Future Commercial Validation

Schools, banks, insurers, municipalities and other organizations are potential future partners or customers. This is a longer-term commercial hypothesis and is not part of the initial user-research phase.

## Potential Revenue Models

- Family Premium — expanded simulations, family challenges and additional content
- School licences — access per class, pupil or institution
- B2B2C partnerships — organizations fund access as part of financial-literacy initiatives
- Scenario packs — modules such as moving out, first car, student life, credit and debt, first job or housing

Before development, separate surveys with players and parents will be used to validate the underlying problem and product assumptions.

The player survey should help establish:
- whether the financial situations feel relevant to the target group
- which situations and decisions are most interesting
- whether the concept sounds engaging
- whether the proposed game mechanics make sense

The parent survey should help establish:


- which functionality parents consider necessary before they would consider paying
- the main concerns parents identify around privacy and AI

**These surveys validate the problem and commercial assumptions, while later user testing evaluates whether the finished solution actually delivers the intended experience**.

Schools, banks, insurers, municipalities and other organizations remain longer-term commercial hypotheses and are not part of the initial research phase.
