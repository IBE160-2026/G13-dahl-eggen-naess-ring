# Market Validation

## Initial Commercial Validation

Parents are the main customer group included in the initial commercial validation.

The research should help establish:

- whether parents see value in a safe financial simulation for teenagers
- whether there is willingness to pay for a future Family version
- what functionality parents would expect before considering payment

The detailed player and parent research plan is documented in [user-research.md](./user-research.md).

---

## Future Commercial Validation

Schools, banks, insurers, municipalities and other organizations are potential future partners or customers.

Banks, insurers, municipalities and other organizations may become future B2B2C partners that fund access through financial-literacy initiatives.

This is a longer-term commercial hypothesis and is not part of the initial user-research phase.

---

## Potential Future Revenue Models

- **Family Premium** — expanded simulations, family challenges and additional content

- **School licences** — access per class, pupil or institution

- **B2B2C partnerships** — banks, insurers or other organizations fund access as part of financial-literacy initiatives. Comparable distribution models are already used by products and initiatives such as EVERFI, Zogo and Nordea's Økonomipeil.

- **Scenario packs** — modules such as moving out, first car, student life, credit and debt, first job or housing
