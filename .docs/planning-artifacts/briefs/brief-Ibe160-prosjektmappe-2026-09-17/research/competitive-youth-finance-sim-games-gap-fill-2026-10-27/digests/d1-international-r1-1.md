## Leads worth chasing

- Payback publisher: check whether the FINRA Foundation funded or co-developed it. Look at the timeforpayback.com Terms/Contact pages, or the "i" info overlay on the site.
- Stukent Mimic Personal Finance: find a live URL and pricing. The current pages return 404, so the product may have been renamed or moved.
- Money Wise Game: check Google Play, the Norwegian App Store (apps.apple.com/no/), and whether a sponsor or bank funded it. Dot Dot Fire is a UK studio.
- Recheck SPENT's mobile version and whether it still works (it may be Flash-era; check the /html/ path).
- The search surfaced the VCEE "Game of Life" personal finance simulation (Virginia Council on Economic Education); it is likely a classroom/paper activity.
- UK: MoneySense (NatWest), Young Money / Money and Pensions Service resources, and the Bank of England's money games. None were found this run.
- Search US state-mandate tools (e.g. "Financial Football" by Visa, the "Bite of Reality" credit-union simulation) and "Real World" events.
