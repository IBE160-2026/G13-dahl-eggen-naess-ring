# Update currency review, 2026-11-23

**Verdict:** Most of the new text holds. One claim is contradicted: strict-mode action models break under FastAPI 0.165 for any field that isn't a plain int, str or bool. The same issue may affect the `Decimal` round-trip if the state models share the strict base. Three items are partly confirmed, with fixes.
