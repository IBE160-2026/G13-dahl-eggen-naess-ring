# Frontend currency review, 2026-11-23

**Verdict:** The frontend stack is sound, and every committed technology still exists and fits. Two version claims are out of date. Next.js 16.4 has replaced 16.3 as the latest feature line (the November security floor is 16.4.5), and Vitest 5 replaced Vitest 4 in September. Several rows are correct but need a reason or a pin written down. The biggest of these is TypeScript: the current major is 7, but 5.x is the right choice, because openapi-typescript and typescript-eslint don't accept 6 or 7 yet. Two points need the sprint-1 spike to confirm them.
